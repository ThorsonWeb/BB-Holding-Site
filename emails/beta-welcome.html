MISSION BRIEFING

What is Battle Beacon?

Battle Beacon is the command deck for tabletop wargamers and the venues they play at. The app connects players to games, opponents, and local stores — and gives venues the tools to fill tables, run events, and build loyal communities.

For players: create and join games, find opponents, message your battle buddies, and track your progress as you earn badges and build your profile. For venues: manage tables, post events, and keep your player base engaged.

As a beta tester, your mission is to break it. Use every feature, push every button, and tell us where the cracks are. That's how we build something worth deploying.

OPERATIONAL GUIDE

How It Works

🎮
Create & Join Games
Post a game with your system, point limit, and preferred venue, or browse open games near you and request to join. Public or private — your call.
👤
Add Friends & Build Your Squad
Search for players, send friend requests, and build a roster of regular opponents. Your squad makes it easier to organise games fast.
💬
Send Messages
Message friends and opponents directly in-app. Coordinate times, venues, and army lists without leaving Battle Beacon.
🔒
Private Games
Set games to private and invite specific players directly. Perfect for club nights, campaigns, and private league matches.
